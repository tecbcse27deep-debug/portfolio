★ Industrial Internship Project · Spring Boot

Online Hotel Booking Platform

A complete hotel & resort booking web application built during my 10-week industrial internship at Ardent Computech Pvt. Ltd. (AICTE NEAT Cell evaluated). Browse hotels & resorts across India, check live room availability, book rooms, pay at a simulated checkout, and manage everything from an admin dashboard. Containerized with Docker and deployed live on Render.

  • Customer registration & login — session-based auth; guests are auto-redirected to login before booking
  • Hotel / resort browsing by city (Delhi, Mumbai, Jaipur, Kochi…) with live room availability
  • Room types & cart — Single / Double / Deluxe booking flow with add-to-cart & remove
  • Admin dashboard — full CRUD to add, edit & delete hotels, rooms and all user bookings
  • Simulated checkout — payment page + booking-confirmation email flow (no real payments)
  • Blog / feedback module — users post reviews, others reply
